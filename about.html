About Us

Building a Reputation, One Surface at a Time

Who We Are

A Dedicated Asphalt & Civils Contractor

Compact Civils is a specialist asphalt surfacing and civil construction company serving commercial, industrial and municipal clients. We plan, prepare and lay every surface with the same standard of care, whether it's a single driveway or a multi-hectare industrial yard.

Over more than a decade, we've built our business on turning up on time, doing the job right the first time, and standing behind the surfaces we lay long after the job is done.
