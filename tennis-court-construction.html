Why the Base Matters Most

A Great Court Is Won or Lost Before the Surface Ever Goes Down

Most court problems — cracking, pooling water, an uneven bounce — trace back to one thing: a base that wasn't prepared properly. That's the part nobody sees once the court is finished, and it's the part we refuse to rush.

We build the whole project ourselves — clearing, levelling, drainage falls, surfacing, line marking, fencing and lighting — so there's no second contractor to blame when something doesn't line up.

  • Engineered base & proper drainage falls
  • Acrylic hard court or synthetic surfacing
  • Precision line marking, included every time
  • Fencing & floodlighting for evening play

Questions We Hear Often

Before You Reach Out

How long does a full build actually take?

It depends on site conditions and scope, but most full builds run a few weeks from the first day on site to a finished, playable court. We'll give you a realistic timeline as part of your written quote.

Can you include fencing and lighting in the same project?

Yes — most clients have us handle everything together. It's quoted as part of the same project, so you're dealing with one team and one timeline, not three separate jobs.

Which surface should I choose?

It comes down to budget, how often the court will be used, and personal preference. We'll walk you through the acrylic and synthetic options on-site so you can decide with real information, not guesswork.

Is the quote actually free, no strings attached?

Yes. We visit your site, assess what's needed, and give you a detailed written quote — there's no cost and no obligation to move forward.
